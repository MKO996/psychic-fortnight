# A/B test: plain names vs game names

**Question:** do people understand the character sheet faster with plain names first (A) or game names first (B)?

| | Version A | Version B |
|---|---|---|
| Big stat name | Bills & essentials | Shield |
| Small name under it | Shield | Bills & essentials |
| Groceries row | Groceries left | Supplies left |
| Next due line | Next payment | Next encounter |

The money figures are identical in both.

## Set up

- **Version A link:** `http://<your-computer-ip>:5173/?v=a`
- **Version B link:** `http://<your-computer-ip>:5173/?v=b`
- Or use Settings → *Usability test: stat names*.
- The top chip shows **Sample · A** or **Sample · B**, so you always know which version is on screen.
- Before each tester: tap **Reset sample**.
- **Alternate the versions:** tester 1 sees A, tester 2 sees B, tester 3 sees A… Aim for at least **5 people per version**. Include people who don't play games and people who are new to budgeting.

## Script (5 minutes, the same for everyone)

Say: *"I'm testing the app, not you. Please think aloud. There are no wrong answers."* Don't explain the screen.

1. *"How much can you spend today?"* (expected: £10.00)
2. *"How much of your money is kept for bills?"* (expected: £350, the Shield / Bills & essentials stat)
3. *"How much is going to savings?"* (expected: £100)
4. *"What's the next payment you need to make?"* (expected: Rent £220)
5. *"How much is left for groceries?"* (expected: £80)

Then ask:

- *"What do you think 'Shield' means here?"*
- *"Did the game words help, get in the way, or not matter?"*
- *"Where did you hesitate?"*

## How to decide

- Look at **Q2 and Q3** first. They depend most on the stat names.
- If B has more wrong answers or clearly slower answers (for example, a few seconds slower on average), keep **A** as the default.
- If both are equally right and fast, and testers liked B more, choose B. You could also start new users on A and offer B later as an option.
- With 5 people per version this is a *direction*, not proof. Look for patterns in the quotes, not just the numbers.
